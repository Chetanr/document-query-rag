# Acme Returns Policy

Customers may return any unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened items can be exchanged within 14 days, subject to inspection.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days
and is free for orders over $100.

## Support

Contact support@acme.example Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm AEST.
